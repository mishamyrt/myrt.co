## Tech lead at <span class="nda">Protected by NDA</span>

Designed and deployed an automated citizen request processing platform for a municipality, consisting of 6 Go services and an on-premise GPU cluster.

Developed a RAG pipeline for generating official replies. Fine-tuned an LLM with LoRA on a corpus of 12,000 request–response pairs to adapt it to the municipality’s writing style.

Implemented a service that clustered similar requests such as utility failures, housing maintenance, and road issues, reducing processing time for high-volume incidents by a factor of four.

Integrated request collection from website comments, Max, and email. Introduced BI dashboards for evaluating contractor reputation based on request statistics.
