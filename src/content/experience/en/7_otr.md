Led a frontend team of 5 within a product team of approximately 60 people and hired 3 developers.

Built a Node.js and Express feature flag service for managing and serving product frontend flags, handling up to approximately 3,000 RPS.

Implemented a PDF exporter in Go that aggregated data from several backend services and rendered an HTML template into a print-ready PDF. Achieved 91% test coverage.
