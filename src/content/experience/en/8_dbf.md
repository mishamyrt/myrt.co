## Team lead and tech lead at [Digital Biz Factory](https://dbfhub.com)

Built and led a team of 9 people—4 backend developers, 2 frontend developers, 2 QA engineers, and an analyst—while developing an employee grading system. Took the project from stagnation to launch in 9 months. Established Definition of Done and Definition of Ready, regular Scrum ceremonies, code review, and sprint demos.

Designed the architecture of 7 Go services. Implemented event-driven analytics with ClickHouse and RabbitMQ. Developed a proof of concept for generating competency matrices from text prompts with an LLM.

Conducted interviews for 11 Angular, 9 React, 8 Go, and 1 Node.js developer positions.

Developed an AI RAG chatbot in Go for civil servants in Kazakhstan. Wrote preprocessing, retrieval, and generation procedures for summarization, contradiction and missing-information detection, and verification against regulations.

Developed the Go backend of an AI RAG chatbot for a financial broker’s mobile app. Implemented investment product selection by risk level, term, currency, and other parameters.

Developed a web app that turned meeting recordings into tasks: it extracted discussed work from daily meeting recordings, displayed it in a UI, and created Jira tickets with one click.

Gave training talks on REST APIs and Git.
